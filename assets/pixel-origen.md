# JembaMD Pixel Edition

Logo y cabecera generados con image_gen integrado, a partir de la referencia visual proporcionada por el usuario. El logo anterior se conserva. Recursos locales: jembamd-pixel.png, jembamd-pixel.ico y noche-pixel.png.

Tipografía: Press Start 2P, descargada del repositorio oficial https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/pressstart2p. Licencia incluida en PressStart2P-OFL.txt. Se usa en títulos; los textos largos usan una fuente monoespaciada del sistema.

## Prompt del logo

Use case: style-transfer. Edit target: image 1 existing JembaMD logo. Style reference: image 2 pixel-art poster. Recreate the logo as authentic crisp low-resolution 16-bit pixel art, designed on an approximately 64x64 pixel grid with hard square pixel clusters. Keep recognizable folded ivory document forming a J and downward mint arrow. Replace the glossy rounded tile with a dark midnight navy square tile with stepped pixel corners and a double turquoise pixel border. Restricted navy, electric blue, ivory and mint palette, a few deliberate pixel highlights, no smooth gradients, no antialiasing, no gloss, no fine text, no university branding, no scenery. The reference poster informs only palette and pixel technique. Strong legibility as a desktop icon. Transparent outside tile.

## Prompt de la cabecera

Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: extra-wide 3:1 panoramic header background for JembaMD, a document converter app with 16-bit pixel-art game styling. Reference image informs pixel technique, midnight blue coastal mood and glowing turquoise highlights only; do not copy the university building, text, insignia or layout. Draw an original tranquil nighttime coastal city with a small illuminated digital archive/library on the RIGHT, teal window lights reflected on wet pavement, distant electric-blue hillside silhouettes, stars, a small pale yellow moon toward upper right and a few palm fronds at the far right edge. LEFT HALF mostly dark navy sky and subtle low silhouette to leave quiet space for an HTML headline. Authentic crisp square pixel clusters, limited navy/blue/turquoise/cream palette, nostalgic adventure game background, rich but restrained detail, no smooth photographic rendering, no letters, no numbers, no UI, no watermark. Wide panoramic landscape, approximately 1536x512.
